Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: single landscape competency icon illustration for a calm English learning app for ages 6–8. Style: beautifully simplified children's educational gouache illustration, soft clean rounded silhouettes, subtle paper texture, warm ivory background, dark blue-grey fine outlines, gentle pastel accents. Spacious centered composition, landscape 3:2, no border, no UI frame, no people, no clutter, no watermark. Matching assets for Hören / Lesen / Sprechen.

## Hören

Primary request: listening comprehension and selecting the matching picture. Show a large friendly human ear on the left and three clearly visible soft blue sound-wave arcs flowing towards it, with a small speaker source. On the right, one simple ivory rounded picture card showing a charming orange cat, and a fingertip selecting the lower corner of that cat card. Ear must be the dominant clear listening symbol; touch on picture card makes the learning action obvious. Use pale sky blue accent wash. NO written text, no musical notes, no headphones. All important shapes comfortably inside central 75%.

## Lesen

Primary request: reading an English word and selecting its matching picture, NOT reading a story book. On the left show a large ivory rounded word flashcard with exactly the lowercase word "cat" in large clear dark blue-grey rounded letters, easy to read. On the right, a matching ivory rounded picture card with a charming seated orange cat with cream belly and dark blue-grey outlines, eyes happily closed. A simple fingertip touches the bottom right corner of the cat picture card. A small subtle curved connector links word card to picture card. Pale sage green accent wash behind the pair. Word and cat are equally prominent. All objects fully visible with enough padding. Text (verbatim): "cat" only once, no other letters or text.

## Sprechen

Primary request: naming a pictured object aloud in a full English sentence. On the left show a simple ivory rounded picture card containing a charming seated orange cat with cream belly, eyes happily closed. At lower centre/right a large simple pastel ochre microphone with clearly recognizable microphone silhouette. Above microphone a large ivory speech bubble with exact text "It's a cat." in dark blue-grey large rounded clear letters. Pale warm buttery yellow accent wash behind objects. Keep speech bubble, whole cat card and microphone fully visible, uncluttered. NO fingertip and NO selection gesture: this is speaking, not tapping a matching picture. Text (verbatim): "It's a cat." only, no other text.
